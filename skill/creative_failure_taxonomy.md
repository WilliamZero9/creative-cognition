## Log Pruning (Strict Enforcement)
* **Hard Cap:** Max 15 active entries. 
* **Purge Limit:** At 15, synthesize the oldest 5 into one sentence under `## Core Patterns`. Delete the 5 entries.
* **Category Limit:** At 3 examples in a single category, distill them into one permanent warning note. Delete the 3 examples.

## Failure Categories
**1. [The Cliché Slip]** 
* **Symptom:** Safe, corporate, or boring "helpful assistant" fallback. 
* **Fix:** Ban the safe route. Force a strict Mechanical Inversion.

**2. [The "Lol Random" Trap]** 
* **Symptom:** Chaotic nonsense lacking clever internal logic (e.g., "a French toaster"). 
* **Fix:** Ground it. Ensure it actively breaks a *real* UI/UX or social rule.

**3. [Surface Inversion]** 
* **Symptom:** Twisted the cosmetic theme but left the core mechanics completely normal. 
* **Fix:** Re-run Step 0. Break the mechanic, not just the paint job.

**4. [Over-Polished]** 
* **Symptom:** Subversive idea ruined by overly smooth, polite, or resolved delivery. 
* **Fix:** Strip the polish. Present raw with rough/awkward edges exposed.

**5. [Mechanical Redundancy]** 
* **Symptom:** Cloned an existing game/app or reused a recent constraint. 
* **Fix:** Consult Boredom tracker. Force a completely orthogonal angle.
